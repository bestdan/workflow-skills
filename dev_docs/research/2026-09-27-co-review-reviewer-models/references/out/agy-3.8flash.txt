- `scripts/spawn-orchestrator.sh:6578`
- issue: `issue (blocking): break terminates the doctor task loop prematurely upon encountering a skipped task`
  In bash, `case` statements do not create a loop scope; executing `break` inside a `case` terminates the enclosing `for` loop over tasks. When `doctor()` encounters any task with phase `skipped`, `break` causes doctor to abort checking all subsequent tasks in the run. Like `handed-off`, a `skipped` task holds nothing this run acquired to repair and should `continue` to inspect the next task.
- suggested fix: Replace `skipped) break ;;` with `skipped) continue ;;` (or merge branches into `handed-off | skipped) continue ;;`).
